Valentin Figueroa

I am the Class of 1958 Career Development Assistant Professor of Political Science at MIT.

I hold a PhD in Political Science from Stanford University (2022). I am from Buenos Aires, Argentina, where I received a B.A. from Universidad de San Andrés (2014) and an M.A. from Universidad Torcuato Di Tella (2017).
